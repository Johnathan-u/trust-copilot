**Goal:** A small MSP can go from the pricing page to a working account with a client in it, and later to paid, without talking to anyone.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. Signup asks for: name, work email, MSP company name, password (or Google/Microsoft sign-in if E-identity supports it). Email is verified before the first export.
2. The trial starts at once, with the limits from TC-36-005 (e.g. 14 days, 2 client workspaces). Limits are enforced by the API, and the UI shows days left and clients used.
3. "Upgrade" opens Stripe Checkout with the MSP prices (TC-35-012). On success, the account becomes paid and client limits follow TC-35-013.
4. When the trial ends without paying, the account goes read-only (it can still export what exists). Nothing is deleted for 30 days. Reminder emails go out 3 days before and on the day.
5. First-login checklist: add branding → add first client (questionnaire-only) → upload first questionnaire.

**Owner step:** needs the Stripe account and live/test prices (TC-35-012). Agents build and test with Stripe test mode.

**Definition of done:** pytest and Playwright pass in Stripe test mode, covering signup → trial → upgrade and signup → trial expiry. Reviewed by a second person.\
Workflow: see SCRUM-35.
