backlog_epic: E35 | tickets: 14 | points: 71 | depends_on_epics: E05, E06, E12, E13, E16, E17, E24, E32 (TC-32-010)

**Objective:** Let MSPs and vCISOs run security questionnaires for many client companies from one account, under their own brand, with reusable answers and per-client billing.

**Why:** Positioning research (2026-11-01) found general questionnaire tools crowded (Vanta, Conveyor, Drata/SafeBase, Loopio, many AI tools); MSP/vCISO multi-client questionnaire response is underserved and TrustCopilot's workspace model already fits it.

**Scope (owner-approved 2026-11-01, nothing else cut):**

1. MSP dashboard: every client workspace in one place, switch between clients, each client's questionnaire status. Builds on TC-32-010 (consultant workspace).
2. White-label: the MSP's logo and domain on exports and the client-facing trust page.
3. Answer templates the MSP reuses across clients, plus ready-made SIG, CAIQ and HECVAT templates.
4. Billing per MSP plus per client workspace.
