Turn TrustCopilot into a broader compliance automation platform like Vanta or Drata. It writes and manages security policies, tracks employee policy acceptance and security training, checks employee devices, and shows overall audit readiness.

**Infrastructure monitoring is already planned elsewhere:**

* **E20 (SCRUM-24):** real connectors for AWS, GCP, Azure, GitHub, GitLab, Google Workspace, Okta, Jira, HRIS and Salesforce.
* **E22 (SCRUM-26):** scheduled monitoring, alerts and remediation.

This epic adds what those don't cover.

**Scope:** every ticket here is outside the frozen scope (docs/acceptance/scope-freeze.md). It needs owner approval and an issue #85 ruling (TC-34-012) before agents start. It is sequenced after the core release and the wedge product work.

Policy and training content must be original. Never copy licensed framework text (see TC-01-007).
