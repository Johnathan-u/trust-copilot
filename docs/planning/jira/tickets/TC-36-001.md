**Goal:** An MSP can add a client and answer its first questionnaire without setting up frameworks, controls or integrations. This is the "questionnaire-first" difference from Vanta and Secureframe.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. When creating a client workspace, the MSP picks "Questionnaire-only" (default) or "Full".
2. In questionnaire-only mode, framework, control and monitoring screens are hidden from navigation and blocked by the API (403 with a clear message). Questionnaires, answer library, evidence, review and export all work.
3. First-run screen for a questionnaire-only client goes straight to "Upload a questionnaire" plus "Add evidence documents". No setup checklist.
4. The MSP can switch a client to "Full" later without losing data. Switching back hides screens again but deletes nothing.
5. The mode is stored per workspace, shows on the MSP dashboard and is covered by the cross-client isolation tests (TC-35-005).

**Definition of done:** pytest and Playwright pass. A new client goes from creation to first generated answers in under 5 minutes in the e2e test. Reviewed by a second person.\
