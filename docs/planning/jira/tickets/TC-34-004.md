Send required policies to every employee:

* at hire, on each new version, and annually
* employees read and accept through a light employee portal (TC-34-005)
* record who accepted which version and when
* send reminders by email or Slack, and escalate overdue cases to admins

**Done when:** acceptance status per employee and per policy can be exported as audit evidence.
