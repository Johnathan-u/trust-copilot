**Goal:** Every Monday the MSP gets one email listing what is due, overdue or waiting for review across all clients, so nothing slips without anyone opening the app.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. The worker sends a weekly digest (default Monday 08:00 in the MSP's time zone) to MSP admins and anyone who opts in.
2. It has three sections: overdue, due in the next 7 days, answers waiting for review. Each row shows client, questionnaire, requester and due date, plus a deep link.
3. Numbers match the MSP dashboard (TC-35-003) because they come from the same query.
4. Per-user settings: off, weekly, or daily. One-click unsubscribe works without logging in.
5. Clients with nothing pending are left out. If everything is clear, a single "all clear" line is sent (or nothing, per setting).

**Definition of done:** tests pass, including the time-zone and "numbers match dashboard" cases. Email renders in the email preview test. Reviewed by a second person.\
