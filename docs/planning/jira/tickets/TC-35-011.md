**Goal:** Recognize the three most common standard questionnaires and pre-map them, so they can be answered once and reused.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. Uploaded SIG (Lite and Core), CAIQ and HECVAT workbooks are detected by version, and every question is mapped to its answer cells without manual mapping.
2. Standard questions link to MSP and client answers by their official question ID, so the same answer fills every future copy.
3. Version changes (new SIG year, HECVAT 4) are flagged with the questions that changed.

**Needs the owner (licensing):** SIG is licensed by Shared Assessments (paid membership required to ship its content); CAIQ is from the Cloud Security Alliance and HECVAT from EDUCAUSE, each with their own terms. Confirm what may be bundled versus only recognized on upload before shipping question text.

**Definition of done:** tests pass on real copies of each questionnaire; licensing decision recorded; reviewed by a second person.\
Workflow: see SCRUM-35.
