**Goal:** Every email a client user receives (invite, review request, approval, password reset, due-date reminder) looks like it came from the MSP, not from TrustCopilot.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. All client-facing email templates use the MSP's logo, colors and company name from TC-35-006. The sender display name is "<MSP name>", and reply-to is the MSP's support address.
2. Footer, signature and links have no TrustCopilot name or logo when the MSP has branding on. Links go to the MSP's portal URL (TC-36-010, or the custom domain from TC-35-008 when set).
3. Optional custom sending domain: the MSP enters a domain, the app shows the SPF/DKIM DNS records, verifies them, and sends from it once verified. Until then it falls back to the shared sender with the MSP display name. Tested against a mocked mail provider.
4. Emails to the MSP's own staff may keep TrustCopilot branding (they are the customer).
5. Plain-text versions are generated for every template.

**Definition of done:** tests pass. A snapshot of every client-facing template is rendered for a branded MSP and checked for the absence of "TrustCopilot". Reviewed by a second person.\
Workflow: see SCRUM-35.
