Window: Nov 20 – Dec 9, 2026 (inside release v1.0, Dec 15)

**Why this epic exists:** Vanta and Secureframe already sell to MSPs. TrustCopilot wins only where they are weak. This epic builds those three advantages on top of E35 (MSP and vCISO edition).

**1. Questionnaire-first.** For the incumbents, questionnaires are an add-on to a full compliance suite, and Vanta needs each client to switch the feature on in its own account. TrustCopilot works with no compliance setup, no client login and one intake point for the MSP.

* TC-36-001 Questionnaire-only client workspaces
* TC-36-002 Zero-touch client provisioning (no client account needed)
* TC-36-003 One intake point: upload a questionnaire and route it to a client
* TC-36-004 Weekly cross-client digest for the MSP

**2. Price.** The suites cost thousands per client per year and go through procurement. TrustCopilot has published prices, a self-serve trial and a clear payback.

* TC-36-005 Owner decision: launch prices and trial limits (human-only)
* TC-36-006 Public MSP pricing page
* TC-36-007 ROI calculator
* TC-36-008 Self-serve MSP signup and trial with no sales call

**3. Branding.** The MSP's brand goes on every surface the client sees, and there is proof that nothing leaks.

* TC-36-009 White-label email notifications
* TC-36-010 Branded client portal (login, review, approval)
* TC-36-011 Remove-branding switch and brand-leak test across every client-facing surface

**Definition of done:** all 11 tickets are Done. A branded MSP can sign up alone, add a client without the client logging in, and take a questionnaire from upload to export. The brand-leak test passes.\
