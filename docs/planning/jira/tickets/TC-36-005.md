blocks: TC-35-012 (Stripe prices), TC-36-006 (pricing page), TC-36-008 (self-serve trial)

**Goal:** Lock the numbers before Stripe products and the pricing page are built. Price is one of the three reasons an MSP would pick TrustCopilot over a compliance suite, so it has to be clearly cheaper and simple to say.

**Decide and write down (as a comment on this ticket)**

1. Base MSP fee per month. Suggested starting point to test: $99–$199/month, including 3 client workspaces.
2. Fee per extra client workspace. Suggested: $15–$30/client/month, with a volume price above 25 clients.
3. Annual discount (suggested 2 months free).
4. Trial: length (suggested 14 days), client workspaces allowed (suggested 2), questionnaire limit, card required or not.
5. What is not included or costs extra (custom domain? SIG content? AI usage cap?).
6. One sentence for the pricing page that compares this with "full compliance suites priced per client per year". Make no claims about named competitors' prices.

**Inputs:** talk to at least 3 MSPs before deciding if possible (see outreach). Check the AI cost per questionnaire from E07 telemetry so the per-client fee covers it at least 5×.

**Definition of done:** the numbers are posted as a comment here, and TC-35-012, TC-36-006 and TC-36-008 are updated to point to that comment.\
