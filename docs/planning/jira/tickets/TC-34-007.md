Assign training automatically from the roster: within 30 days of hire, annually, and when a module changes.

* send reminders and escalations through E19
* show a completion dashboard by employee, team and module
* map completion records as evidence to the relevant controls, such as SOC 2 security-awareness criteria and ISO 27001:2022 Annex A 6.3

**Done when:** an auditor can export who completed which module and when, with quiz scores.
