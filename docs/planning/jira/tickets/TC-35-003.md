**Goal:** One screen where an MSP sees every client and the state of each client's questionnaires.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. Table of client workspaces with: open questionnaires, answers awaiting review, approved, exported, nearest due date, overdue count, last activity.
2. Sort and filter by client, status, due date and overdue; search by client name.
3. Clicking a client or a questionnaire opens it inside that client's workspace (uses TC-35-004 switcher).
4. Counts come from the same review states as E12 (no separate calculation that can drift); numbers are scoped to clients the viewer can access.
5. Works at 360, 768 and 1440 px and by keyboard.

**Definition of done:** Playwright spec passes; accessibility check has no serious violations; reviewed by a second person.\
Workflow: see SCRUM-35.
