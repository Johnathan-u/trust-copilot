## Gaps
1. Stop document and questionnaire uploads before the rest of the body is read. Replace `file.file.read()` at `file_service.py:64` with a counted read that raises 413 at `max_upload_bytes` and does not keep the extra bytes. Use the same helper for trust intake (`trust_requests.py:210-213`) and delete or align the unused `upload_trust_request_attachment` (`file_service.py:99-114`).
2. Make the proxy return 413 above 50 MiB without buffering the body. Production Caddy (`Caddyfile.production:34-37`) has no `request_body` limit. The Next.js proxy (`route.ts:90-94`) copies the entire request into an `ArrayBuffer`. The limit has to follow `MAX_UPLOAD_BYTES`, not a second hardcoded number.
3. Prove a rejected or interrupted upload leaves nothing behind. The test should post just under and just over the limit to `/api/documents/upload`, `/api/questionnaires/upload`, and `/api/trust-requests/submit`, expect 413, and assert no document, questionnaire, job, or stored object. Include a client disconnect during the body. The existing enqueue-failure cleanup tests do not cover this.
4. Add `apps/api/tests/release/test_upload_stream.py`, `artifacts/acceptance/TC-07-006/`, and `docs/release/tickets/TC-07-006.md`. The backlog command is `python -m pytest tests/release/test_upload_stream.py -q`.
