## Acceptance criteria

1. `compliance_coverage._evidence_is_current` calls `evidence_qualification.evidence_qualifies` and keeps its own reason codes. Coverage and the evidence gate then can't drift apart. A test fails if the two rules diverge.
2. `docs/release/tickets/TC-21-007.md` explains that coverage "stale" means the evidence has expired, while the control engine's "stale" means the evidence qualifies but is more than 180 days old. If the two should mean the same thing, a product ticket is opened for that.
