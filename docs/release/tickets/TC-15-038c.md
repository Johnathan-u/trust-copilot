## Review of #262

`parse_evidence` stays the empty branch in `run_job`. It does not download or parse a file, and its row keeps "Progress not reported". A real evidence-parse job would be its own ticket.

Parse and index publish `{generated, total}` on a separate short session while the job is `running` and the lease owner still matches, at most about once a second, and again at the end. The job transaction's own commit keeps the last pair. Export writes the question total only when the file is finished. The workbook is produced in one step, so that pair is a completion fact, not a mid-run counter.
